# Canonical Working Content

Status: Planning / Pre-development. Language: English (US). Currency: USD.

This is the editorial source for the future website. It preserves the blueprint's canonical dishes and incorporates the approved hero wording, hours, identity, and concise disclosure strategy. Those decisions are finalized; only explicitly marked additions and remaining supporting copy need review. No missing prices or biography claims have been invented.

## Content rules

- Use concrete food descriptions and short, focused paragraphs, generally two to four sentences.
- Maintain one canonical name, description, and price per dish across Home and Menu.
- Use whole-dollar USD prices consistently, such as $17 and $36.
- Use US spelling, punctuation, and time conventions.
- Never claim awards, press coverage, real customer reviews, locally sourced ingredients, or real business outcomes without a basis.
- Do not add an address, operating-business map pin, reservation promise, or tax/gratuity policy.
- Dietary labels require complete ingredient review; omit them by default.
- Use one concise shared-footer disclosure, contextual Contact and chef notices, and concept metadata. Do not repeat large FICTIONAL, DEMO, or NOT REAL labels across visual sections.

## Contact form copy

Canonical Contact demonstration notice, shown once before input and within the sample-details/form context:

> Portfolio demonstration — messages are not sent or stored.

Required-field helper: **Fields marked required must be completed.**

| Field | Label | Required/error wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Full Name (required) | Please enter your full name. |
| Email | Email Address (required) | Please enter a valid email address. |
| Phone | Phone Number (optional) | Optional; no required-field error |
| Subject | Subject (required) | Please enter a subject. |
| Message | Message (required) | Please enter a message. |

Subject is a required text input, with no placeholder or option list.

Proposed length helper: **Maximum 2,000 characters.**

Proposed length error: **Please keep your message to 2,000 characters or fewer.**

Submit CTA: **Send Message**, within the clearly introduced demonstration form. Use the single notice above rather than adding a disclaimer beside every control.

Submitting label: **Simulating submission…**

Lifecycle: `idle → validating → submitting → success`. Validation is local and immediate; it does not need an artificial waiting label or delay. Invalid input returns to an editable state with values preserved, accessible error messages, and first-invalid-field focus when appropriate. Valid input shows brief simulated progress, then the completion message below. No network request, persistence, or fake email delivery occurs.

Validation summary: **Please review the highlighted fields.** Do not rely on highlighting alone; show the individual text errors.

Completion:

> Demo complete. Your message was not sent or stored.

Do not say Message sent, promise a reply, or show a fake server error. No character limits or counter are enforced. The proposed Message limit above remains unapproved. After valid simulation, clear all fields, restore editing, and retain success until new entry clears it. Invalid submissions preserve values.
